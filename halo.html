Halo

Halo is a first-person shooter video game franchise created by Bungie and now managed and developed by 343 Industries, a subsidiary of Xbox Game Studios. The series centers on an interstellar war between humanity and an alliance of aliens known as the Covenant. The Covenant, led by their religious leaders called the Prophets, worship an ancient civilization known as the Forerunners, who perished in combat with the parasitic Flood. The main protagonist of the series is Master Chief Petty Officer John-117, a supersoldier known as a Spartan, and his artificial intelligence companion Cortana.

The Halo franchise has been critically acclaimed for its engaging storylines, innovative gameplay mechanics, and multiplayer features. It has spawned numerous sequels, spin-offs, novels, comics, and other media. The series has also been praised for its contributions to the first-person shooter genre and its influence on gaming culture.
